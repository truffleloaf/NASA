# Data extensions

- Arbitrary coordinates and Apollo/CLPS sites need new ephemerides and terrain horizon profiles. Proximity to a representative site does not validate its visibility at another point.
- Higher-resolution local DEMs are required to assess boulders, steep crater slopes, landing hazards and metre-scale mast clearance.
- Permanent shadow classification requires an appropriate long-term illumination analysis; a dark crater in the current renderer is not evidence of permanent shadow.
- Radio-link budgets and relay assets need new inputs; geometric Earth visibility alone is not communications availability.
